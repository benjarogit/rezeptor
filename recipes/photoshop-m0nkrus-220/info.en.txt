# Adobe Photoshop CC 2021 (m0nkrus 22.0.0.35)

Author: benjarogit
Version: 22.0.0.35 (offline installer, tested m0nkrus pack)

## Summary
Adobe Photoshop CC 2021 on Proton-GE — quiet background install (offline installer).
Separate recipe for the m0nkrus pack **Photoshop.2021** (ISO-only) — not the standard `photoshop` recipe and not the **22.1.1.138** pack (`photoshop-m0nkrus`).

## What to search for (BYOS)
Rezeptor provides **no downloads and no links**. Obtain the pack yourself. Search terms:

• Adobe Photoshop 2021 (v22.0) Multilingual
• Adobe.Photoshop.2021.Multilingual
• Photoshop.2021
• rutracker
• m0nkrus

## Recommended source
Pick the **pack folder** (e.g. `Photoshop.2021`) or the ISO:

• `Adobe.Photoshop.2021.Multilingual.iso` — offline installer (under `Adobe 2021/`: Set-up.exe + packages/)
• optional: `m0nkrus.nfo`, checksums (.md5 / .sha / .sfv)

This pack has **no** Neural Filters / missing_libs / GenP (unlike 22.1.1.138).

## Requirements
• Proton-GE (used automatically)
• Pack folder with Adobe `.iso` (or mounted/extracted Set-up.exe + packages/)
• About 15 GB free disk space

## Install
1. Choose “Install”
2. Pick pack folder `Photoshop.2021` or the ISO (often suggested under Downloads)
3. Check the data folder (default: ~/.local/share/wine-software/photoshop-m0nkrus-220)

## Use
• “Launch” in the launcher or the desktop shortcut
• First start may take 1–2 minutes

## Notes
• Other packs: standard `photoshop` or m0nkrus **22.1.1.138** (`photoshop-m0nkrus`)
• Blurry fonts: run Repair or check the log
• Logs: ~/.local/share/wine-software/logs/
