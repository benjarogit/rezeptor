# Master PDF Editor 5

Offline MSI on **Proton-GE** (pinned in `core/runtime.lock`).

## Source (BYOS)

- Pack folder with `MasterPDFEditor-setup-qt6-x64.msi` (Qt6 x64) — Rezeptor ships neither the setup nor extras
- Optional: `fix/MasterPDFEditor.exe` in the same pack — applied after MSI if present

## Notes

- After install, launch uses the prefix path (`Code Industry/Master PDF Editor 5`).
- Without a fix, the MSI build remains (license/trial depends on the pack).
- Winetricks: `vcrun2019`, `corefonts` (Qt6).
- Uninstall removes the prefix and desktop entries under the recipe `data_root`.
